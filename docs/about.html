ABOUT VIRLA

Fitness should be easier to access.

Virla is a fitness training platform created to make professional, personalised training more convenient for customers. Virla brings customers and trainers together through a simple digital experience for discovering and booking fitness sessions.

OUR APPROACH

Professional training. Simple experience.

We believe people are more likely to stay consistent when fitness fits naturally into their daily lives. Virla is built around convenience, trainer-led sessions and clear customer choice.

01

Customer First

We focus on making it straightforward for customers to understand services, choose suitable trainers and manage their sessions.

02

Trainer Led

Virla is designed around professional trainers delivering personalised fitness guidance and sessions.

03

Built for Consistency

Convenient scheduling and structured sessions help customers make training part of their routine.
