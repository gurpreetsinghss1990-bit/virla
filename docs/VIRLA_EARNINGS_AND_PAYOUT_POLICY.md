OFFICIAL EARNINGS & PAYOUT POLICY
VIRLA — EARNINGS & PAYOUT POLICY
Version: 1.0  |  Applicable To: VIRLA Partner Coaches / Trainers

1. PURPOSE
This policy explains how VIRLA Partner Coaches earn money through the VIRLA platform, how weekly payouts are calculated and processed, the requirements for receiving payments, applicable incentives, payment verification requirements, and circumstances in which earnings may be reviewed, adjusted, held, or deferred.

By accepting this policy, the Trainer confirms that they have read, understood, and agreed to comply with the VIRLA Earnings & Payout Policy.

2. INDEPENDENT PARTNER STATUS
VIRLA Partner Coaches provide fitness, wellness and related services as independent partners of the VIRLA platform.

Acceptance onto the VIRLA platform does not guarantee:

Any minimum number of sessions.
Any minimum number of bookings.
Any minimum level of earnings.
Any fixed number of clients.
Trainers are responsible for maintaining their availability, professional standards, certifications and compliance with all VIRLA policies.

3. BASE SESSION EARNINGS
The current standard base earning for an eligible and successfully completed VIRLA session is:

₹850 PER COMPLETED ELIGIBLE SESSION

Additional incentives may be available from time to time.

Incentives may be based on factors including, but not limited to:

Number of completed sessions.
Trainer performance.
Client satisfaction.
Attendance and reliability.
Customer ratings and feedback.
Session quality.
Special VIRLA campaigns.
Promotional programs.
Other performance criteria determined by VIRLA.
Detailed incentive structures, eligibility requirements and applicable amounts may be communicated separately by VIRLA.

A booking does not automatically constitute an earning.

A session must satisfy VIRLA's eligibility requirements before it becomes payable.

4. WEEKLY PAYOUT CYCLE
VIRLA operates a weekly payout cycle.

The standard earning period is:

MONDAY 00:00 → SUNDAY 23:59

Eligible earnings generated during this period will ordinarily be processed for payment on the following MONDAY.

Example:

If a Trainer joins VIRLA on Thursday and completes eligible sessions from Thursday through Sunday, those eligible earnings will normally be included in the following Monday payout.

If Monday is a bank holiday, public holiday, banking disruption, payment-processing disruption, or other operational interruption, VIRLA may process the payment on the next available banking day.

5. PAYOUT ELIGIBILITY
A Trainer becomes eligible for payout only for sessions and earnings that are successfully recorded and approved as payable under VIRLA's systems and policies.

VIRLA may review session records before processing payouts.

Eligibility may depend on:

Successful completion of the session.
Correct session attendance records.
Compliance with VIRLA policies.
No unresolved material dispute.
No fraud or suspected fraudulent activity.
Correct payout information.
Compliance with Trainer conduct requirements.
Any applicable cancellation/no-show rules.
Any applicable client dispute or refund.
Other legitimate payout requirements communicated by VIRLA.
6. BANK ACCOUNT AND UPI REQUIREMENTS
Every Trainer must provide accurate payment information through the official VIRLA application or other officially designated VIRLA payment-registration process.

The payment information must belong to the Trainer.

The following rules apply:

The bank account must be held in the Trainer's own name.
The UPI ID must belong to the Trainer.
The bank-account holder name must correspond with the Trainer's verified identity.
The UPI account must correspond with the Trainer's verified identity.
Trainers may not nominate another person's bank account.
Trainers may not nominate another person's UPI ID.
Trainers may not request payment to a family member's, friend's, employee's, company's or any other third-party account unless VIRLA has expressly approved such arrangement in writing and applicable law permits it.
VIRLA may verify payment information before processing a payout.
VIRLA may request additional documentation or verification where reasonably required.
The Trainer is responsible for ensuring that all payment information submitted to VIRLA remains accurate and current.

7. PAYMENT INFORMATION MISMATCH
If the bank account, UPI ID, account-holder name or other payout information is:

Incorrect;
Incomplete;
Invalid;
Suspended;
Unverifiable; or
Inconsistent with the Trainer's verified identity,
VIRLA may pause the affected payout until the payment information is corrected and successfully verified.

If the corrected information is received after the applicable payout-processing cutoff, the affected amount may be moved to the next scheduled Monday payout cycle.

Incorrect payment information does not automatically result in forfeiture of legitimate earnings.

8. TAXES AND STATUTORY DEDUCTIONS
All Trainer payments are subject to applicable Indian laws, tax requirements, withholding requirements and other statutory deductions, where applicable.

VIRLA may deduct or withhold amounts where required by applicable law.

The final amount received by the Trainer may therefore differ from the gross amount shown in the VIRLA earnings record.

Trainers are responsible for their own tax compliance and reporting obligations to the extent applicable to them.

9. PHYSICAL TRAINING AND VIRLA CERTIFICATION
Before becoming an active VIRLA Partner Coach, a Trainer may be required to attend VIRLA's designated physical training and certification process.

The Trainer may be required to:

Attend the required physical training.
Complete the required practical training.
Demonstrate appropriate technical knowledge.
Demonstrate safe client-handling practices.
Meet VIRLA's professional standards.
Successfully complete the required assessment.
Complete any required verification process.
Only Trainers who successfully satisfy VIRLA's certification requirements may be approved as active VIRLA Partner Coaches.

Completion of the application alone does not guarantee certification or activation.

10. TRAINER EQUIPMENT AND VIRLA BRAND REQUIREMENTS
Active VIRLA Partner Coaches may be required to use specified VIRLA-branded apparel, equipment or accessories as part of VIRLA's professional and brand standards.

These may include:

VIRLA Trainer T-shirt.
VIRLA Water Bottle.
VIRLA Towel.
VIRLA-approved personal-care or branding items.
Other equipment or accessories required by VIRLA.
VIRLA may introduce, modify, replace or discontinue required equipment or branding standards from time to time.

Where applicable, VIRLA will communicate the relevant item, purchase requirement, pricing and applicable procedure separately.

Trainers must use required VIRLA-branded items in accordance with the applicable Trainer and Brand Guidelines.

11. PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT
Every VIRLA Partner Coach must maintain professional, respectful and safe behaviour while interacting with VIRLA clients.

Trainers must:

Treat clients respectfully.
Maintain appropriate professional boundaries.
Follow VIRLA safety requirements.
Follow VIRLA Partner Terms and Professional Standards.
Obtain explicit client consent before providing physical assistance.
Avoid unnecessary physical contact.
Maintain appropriate communication with clients.
Protect client privacy and personal information.
Follow all applicable VIRLA policies.
Report serious safety or misconduct concerns through the appropriate VIRLA channel.
VIRLA maintains a zero-tolerance approach toward serious inappropriate behaviour, harassment, exploitation, abuse, threats, discrimination or conduct that compromises client safety.

A serious violation may result in immediate suspension while the matter is investigated and may result in termination of the Trainer's VIRLA partnership.

12. CLIENT SAFETY
Trainer safety and client safety are fundamental requirements of the VIRLA platform.

A Trainer must not:

Perform activities outside their appropriate qualification or competence.
Ignore known safety concerns.
Force or pressure a client to perform an exercise or activity.
Provide physical assistance without appropriate client consent.
Engage in inappropriate physical or verbal behaviour.
Misuse a client's personal information.
Engage in behaviour that creates a foreseeable risk to the client.
Any serious safety concern may result in immediate review, temporary suspension or other action by VIRLA.

13. CLIENT CONFIDENTIALITY
Trainers may receive personal information about VIRLA clients for the purpose of providing VIRLA services.

Such information must only be used for legitimate VIRLA-related purposes.

Trainers must not:

Sell client information.
Share client information without authorization.
Use client information for unrelated commercial purposes.
Contact clients for unauthorized private business.
Store or distribute client information improperly.
These obligations may continue after a Trainer leaves the VIRLA platform where required by applicable law or agreement.

14. NO DIRECT CLIENT CIRCUMVENTION
VIRLA clients introduced, assigned, booked or connected through the VIRLA platform must not be diverted away from VIRLA for private or unauthorized off-platform sessions.

A Trainer must not:

Ask a VIRLA client to pay the Trainer directly for a VIRLA-related session.
Accept cash, UPI, bank transfer or any other direct payment from a VIRLA client for an unauthorized VIRLA-related session.
Arrange private sessions with VIRLA clients outside the VIRLA platform without VIRLA's prior authorization.
Share personal payment information for the purpose of bypassing VIRLA.
Encourage a VIRLA client to cancel a VIRLA booking and deal directly with the Trainer.
Use VIRLA customer information to establish an unauthorized private commercial relationship.
Attempt to move VIRLA clients away from the VIRLA platform for the purpose of avoiding VIRLA fees, processes or controls.
This requirement exists to protect clients, Trainers and the VIRLA platform.

15. CONSEQUENCES OF CLIENT CIRCUMVENTION
If VIRLA reasonably determines that a Trainer has attempted or engaged in unauthorized client circumvention, VIRLA may take appropriate action, including:

Investigation of the matter.
Temporary suspension of the Trainer account.
Restriction of access to new bookings.
Review of affected earnings.
Withholding of disputed or affected amounts pending investigation.
Recovery or adjustment of amounts attributable to the violation where legally permissible.
Termination of the Trainer partnership.
Permanent removal from the VIRLA platform.
Other contractual or legal remedies available to VIRLA.
Any action will be considered in accordance with the circumstances, available evidence, applicable VIRLA agreements and applicable law.
