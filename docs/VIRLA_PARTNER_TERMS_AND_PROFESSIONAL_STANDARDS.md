OFFICIAL PARTNER TERMS & STANDARDS
VIRLA — PARTNER TERMS & PROFESSIONAL STANDARDS
Version: 1.0  |  Applicable To: VIRLA Partner Coaches / Trainers

1. PURPOSE
These VIRLA Partner Terms & Professional Standards establish the rules, responsibilities, professional expectations, safety requirements and operating standards applicable to every VIRLA Partner Coach.

The purpose of these Terms is to:

Protect VIRLA clients.
Protect VIRLA Partner Coaches.
Maintain a consistent professional standard.
Protect VIRLA's brand and reputation.
Establish clear expectations for Trainer conduct.
Ensure safe and reliable fitness and wellness services.
Prevent misuse of the VIRLA platform.
Protect client information.
Establish consequences for serious or repeated policy violations.
By accepting these Terms, the Trainer confirms that they have read, understood and agreed to comply with these requirements.

2. VIRLA PARTNER STATUS
VIRLA Partner Coaches provide services through the VIRLA platform as independent partners, subject to the applicable VIRLA agreements and policies.

Acceptance as a VIRLA Partner Coach does not guarantee:

Any minimum number of bookings.
Any minimum number of sessions.
Any minimum income.
Any fixed number of clients.
Any particular working hours.
Any particular geographic area.
A Trainer's continued access to the VIRLA platform is subject to continued compliance with VIRLA's requirements.

3. TRAINER ELIGIBILITY
A person applying to become a VIRLA Partner Coach must provide truthful and accurate information during the application and verification process.

Depending on VIRLA's requirements, the Trainer may be required to provide:

Full legal name.
Mobile number.
Date of birth.
Address or location information.
Identity documentation.
Qualifications or certifications.
Fitness or professional experience.
Bank and UPI information.
Profile photograph.
Other information reasonably required by VIRLA.
VIRLA may verify information provided during onboarding.

Providing false, misleading, forged, manipulated or materially incomplete information may result in rejection, suspension or termination of the application or Trainer account.

4. IDENTITY VERIFICATION
Every Trainer must maintain an accurate and verifiable identity on the VIRLA platform.

The Trainer must not:

Use another person's identity.
Allow another person to use their Trainer account.
Submit another person's identity document as their own.
Provide false information during verification.
Manipulate or alter verification documents.
Attempt to bypass VIRLA's identity verification process.
VIRLA may request additional verification where reasonably required.

If VIRLA is unable to reasonably verify the Trainer's identity, VIRLA may temporarily restrict access until verification is completed.

5. TRAINER CERTIFICATION
A Trainer may be required to successfully complete VIRLA's physical training, practical assessment and certification process before becoming eligible to provide sessions through the platform.

The Trainer must maintain any qualifications, certifications or other professional requirements applicable to the services they provide.

A Trainer must not represent themselves as qualified or certified in an area where they do not possess the appropriate qualification, certification, training or competence.

6. PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT
Every VIRLA Partner Coach must conduct themselves professionally when interacting with VIRLA clients, other Trainers, VIRLA employees, representatives and partners.

Trainers must:

Be respectful.
Be courteous.
Communicate professionally.
Maintain appropriate boundaries.
Arrive prepared for sessions.
Follow VIRLA procedures.
Respect client preferences.
Respect client privacy.
Follow applicable safety requirements.
Maintain professional appearance.
Use appropriate language.
Avoid behaviour that could reasonably make a client feel unsafe, threatened, harassed or uncomfortable.
Professional conduct is required both during sessions and in other VIRLA-related communications.

7. ZERO TOLERANCE FOR SERIOUS MISCONDUCT
VIRLA does not tolerate serious misconduct.

This includes, but is not limited to:

Sexual harassment.
Sexual misconduct.
Unwanted sexual behaviour.
Threats.
Intimidation.
Physical abuse.
Verbal abuse.
Discrimination.
Harassment.
Bullying.
Stalking.
Exploitation.
Deliberate intimidation.
Serious inappropriate physical contact.
Fraud.
Theft.
Deliberate misuse of client information.
Any conduct that creates a serious safety risk.
Serious misconduct may result in immediate suspension while VIRLA reviews the matter and may result in permanent removal from the platform.

8. PHYSICAL CONTACT AND CLIENT CONSENT
Trainers must respect client boundaries at all times.

Physical assistance should only be provided where:

It is reasonably necessary for technique correction or safety; and
The client has provided appropriate consent.
Trainers must avoid unnecessary physical contact.

A Trainer must immediately respect a client's request to stop physical contact.

A Trainer must never use physical contact as a means of intimidation, punishment, harassment or inappropriate behaviour.

9. CLIENT SAFETY
Client safety is a fundamental requirement of every VIRLA session.

Trainers must:

Conduct sessions responsibly.
Consider the client's stated limitations.
Use appropriate exercise techniques.
Avoid knowingly unsafe exercises.
Follow VIRLA safety procedures.
Stop or modify an activity where a reasonable safety concern arises.
Encourage clients to seek appropriate professional medical advice where necessary.
Report serious incidents through the appropriate VIRLA process.
Trainers must not knowingly provide medical diagnosis or medical treatment unless separately qualified and legally permitted to do so.

10. TRAINER COMPETENCE
A Trainer must only provide services that fall within their training, qualifications, experience and VIRLA-approved scope of services.

A Trainer must not falsely claim expertise in:

Medical treatment.
Physiotherapy.
Rehabilitation.
Nutrition.
Mental-health treatment.
Medical diagnosis.
Any specialised professional field for which the Trainer does not possess appropriate qualifications.
Where a client's needs appear to exceed the Trainer's competence, the Trainer should recommend that the client seek an appropriately qualified professional.

11. CLIENT COMMUNICATION
Trainers must communicate with clients primarily through VIRLA-approved channels where required by VIRLA.

Trainers must not use client communication to:

Harass the client.
Pressure the client.
Sell unrelated products or services without authorization.
Solicit private business.
Request inappropriate personal information.
Request inappropriate photographs or videos.
Make inappropriate personal or sexual comments.
Circumvent VIRLA's platform.
12. CLIENT PRIVACY AND CONFIDENTIALITY
Client information obtained through VIRLA must be treated as confidential.

This may include:

Name.
Mobile number.
Address.
Location.
Fitness information.
Personal preferences.
Health-related information shared for session purposes.
Photographs.
Videos.
Booking information.
Payment information.
Communications.
Any other personal information obtained through VIRLA.
Trainers must not disclose, sell, publish, share or misuse client information.

Client information may only be used for legitimate purposes connected with providing VIRLA services and other purposes expressly authorized by VIRLA or the client, as applicable.

13. PHOTOGRAPHS AND VIDEOS
Trainers must not photograph, record or publish a VIRLA client without appropriate permission and compliance with VIRLA's applicable media and privacy requirements.

A Trainer must not:

Secretly record a client.
Photograph a client without appropriate consent.
Publish client images without authorization.
Use client images for personal advertising without authorization.
Upload client content to social media without appropriate permission.
14. BOOKINGS
Trainers must maintain accurate availability on the VIRLA platform.

A Trainer should accept only bookings they genuinely expect to be able to complete.

Once a Trainer accepts a booking, the Trainer is expected to make reasonable efforts to attend and complete the session.

Repeated cancellations, avoidable no-shows or unreliable attendance may affect:

Platform eligibility.
Booking access.
Incentives.
Performance status.
Retainer eligibility, where applicable.
Continued Trainer status.
15. TRAINER NO-SHOWS
A Trainer must not deliberately fail to attend an accepted booking.

If an emergency or genuine unavoidable circumstance occurs, the Trainer must notify VIRLA as soon as reasonably possible.

Repeated or unexplained no-shows may result in:

Investigation.
Warning.
Restriction of bookings.
Loss of applicable incentives.
Suspension.
Termination.
16. CLIENT CANCELLATIONS AND DISPUTES
Trainers must follow VIRLA's applicable cancellation and dispute procedures.

Trainers must not independently alter, falsify or manipulate booking records.

If a client disputes a session, the Trainer must cooperate with VIRLA's review.

The Trainer must provide truthful information and any relevant evidence reasonably requested by VIRLA.

17. SESSION RECORDING AND ATTENDANCE
Trainers must accurately record session attendance and completion through VIRLA's designated system.

A Trainer must not:

Mark an uncompleted session as completed.
Ask a client to falsely confirm attendance.
Create duplicate session records.
Manipulate session timestamps.
Falsify session information.
Attempt to obtain payment for a session that was not legitimately completed.
Any deliberate manipulation may be treated as a serious policy violation.

18. VIRLA PLATFORM USE
Trainers must use the VIRLA platform responsibly.

Trainers must not:

Attempt to hack or compromise VIRLA systems.
Access another Trainer's account.
Access another user's private information without authorization.
Circumvent security controls.
Manipulate bookings.
Manipulate earnings.
Create fraudulent accounts.
Create duplicate accounts to obtain benefits.
Use automated methods to manipulate the platform.
Interfere with platform operations.
19. ACCOUNT SECURITY
A Trainer is responsible for protecting their VIRLA account credentials and access.

The Trainer must not knowingly share their account credentials with another person.

If the Trainer suspects unauthorized access, they must notify VIRLA promptly.

VIRLA may temporarily restrict an account where there is a reasonable security concern.

20. NO ACCOUNT SHARING
A VIRLA Trainer account is personal to the approved Trainer.

Another person may not provide sessions using the Trainer's account.

A Trainer must not:

Send another person to conduct a session under their account.
Allow another person to use their Trainer profile.
Allow another person to accept bookings on their behalf without VIRLA's authorization.
Transfer their Trainer account to another person.
Any unauthorized substitution may result in immediate review and suspension.

21. NO DIRECT CLIENT CIRCUMVENTION
VIRLA clients obtained, introduced, assigned or connected through the VIRLA platform must remain within the VIRLA platform for VIRLA-related services unless VIRLA has expressly authorized otherwise.

A Trainer must not:

Take a VIRLA client privately for a VIRLA-related service.
Request direct payment from a VIRLA client.
Ask a client to pay by cash, UPI or bank transfer outside the VIRLA system.
Encourage a client to cancel a VIRLA booking and deal directly with the Trainer.
Share personal payment details for the purpose of bypassing VIRLA.
Use VIRLA client information to create an unauthorized private business relationship.
Redirect VIRLA clients to another platform or business for the purpose of avoiding VIRLA's processes.
This rule exists to protect the VIRLA client relationship, platform integrity and Trainer ecosystem.

22. DIRECT CLIENT PAYMENT
A Trainer must not accept unauthorized direct payments from a VIRLA client for a VIRLA-related session.

All VIRLA-related payments must be processed through the VIRLA-approved payment and booking process.

If a client offers direct payment, the Trainer must refuse the payment and direct the client to the VIRLA platform.

23. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
Trainers must avoid situations where their personal interests materially interfere with their responsibilities to VIRLA clients or the VIRLA platform.

Trainers must not use their VIRLA position to obtain unauthorized personal financial benefits from VIRLA clients.

Potential conflicts should be disclosed to VIRLA where reasonably necessary.

24. SOLICITATION OF CLIENTS
Trainers must not use the VIRLA platform or VIRLA client information to solicit clients for unrelated personal businesses without authorization.

This includes solicitation for:

Private personal training.
Other fitness businesses.
Competing platforms.
Paid products.
Unapproved services.
Unapproved memberships.
Unapproved commercial activities.
25. VIRLA BRAND AND REPRESENTATION
Trainers represent themselves as VIRLA Partner Coaches when providing VIRLA services.

Trainers must use the VIRLA brand responsibly.

Trainers must not:

Make unauthorized statements on behalf of VIRLA.
Claim to be an employee, director or legal representative of VIRLA unless expressly authorized.
Make promises about VIRLA services that have not been approved.
Misrepresent VIRLA pricing.
Misrepresent VIRLA policies.
Use VIRLA branding for unrelated commercial activity without authorization.
Publish confidential VIRLA information.
26. SOCIAL MEDIA
Trainers may maintain personal social-media accounts.

However, Trainers must not:

Misrepresent themselves as an official VIRLA spokesperson.
Publish confidential VIRLA information.
Publish client information without appropriate authorization.
Use VIRLA branding in a misleading manner.
Make false claims about VIRLA.
Use VIRLA client relationships to promote unauthorized personal businesses.
Where a Trainer identifies themselves as a VIRLA Partner Coach, their public conduct should remain consistent with VIRLA's professional standards.

27. TRAINER EQUIPMENT AND APPEARANCE
Trainers must comply with VIRLA's applicable Trainer appearance and equipment requirements.

Where required, Trainers must use approved VIRLA-branded apparel or equipment.

Trainers should maintain:

Clean and professional clothing.
Appropriate personal hygiene.
Professional grooming.
Appropriate footwear.
Required VIRLA-branded items.
A professional appearance suitable for client-facing services.
28. CLIENT GIFTS, MONEY AND PERSONAL REQUESTS
Trainers must not pressure clients to provide gifts, money, personal benefits or favours.

If a client offers a gift or benefit, the Trainer must comply with VIRLA's applicable policy regarding such situations.

Trainers must never use their position to exploit a client's trust.

29. ALCOHOL, DRUGS AND IMPAIRMENT
A Trainer must not conduct a VIRLA session while impaired by:

Alcohol.
Recreational drugs.
Misused medication.
Any other substance or condition that materially affects their ability to safely perform the session.
A Trainer must not bring or use prohibited substances during a VIRLA session.

30. CRIMINAL OR SERIOUS LEGAL ISSUES
Trainers must not engage in criminal conduct connected with their VIRLA activities.

Where VIRLA becomes aware of a serious allegation or legal matter that may materially affect client safety, Trainer eligibility or platform integrity, VIRLA may take appropriate interim action while the matter is reviewed.

31. INCIDENT REPORTING
Trainers should promptly report serious incidents to VIRLA.

Examples include:

Client injury.
Safety incident.
Serious complaint.
Harassment allegation.
Threat.
Unauthorized payment.
Fraud.
Account compromise.
Loss of client property.
Serious misconduct.
Any event that may materially affect client safety or VIRLA.
Trainers must cooperate honestly with VIRLA's investigation.

32. COOPERATION WITH INVESTIGATIONS
When VIRLA reasonably investigates a complaint, dispute, safety incident, fraud concern or policy violation, the Trainer must cooperate.

The Trainer may be asked to provide:

A written explanation.
Booking information.
Relevant communications.
Session details.
Supporting evidence.
Other information reasonably necessary for the investigation.
The Trainer must not deliberately destroy, manipulate or conceal relevant information.

33. TEMPORARY SUSPENSION
VIRLA may temporarily suspend or restrict a Trainer's access where reasonably necessary to:

Protect client safety.
Investigate serious allegations.
Protect the platform.
Prevent fraud.
Protect confidential information.
Investigate account security concerns.
Prevent continued policy violations.
Temporary suspension does not necessarily mean that a final violation has been established.

VIRLA may complete an appropriate review before determining the final outcome.

34. DISCIPLINARY ACTION
Depending on the seriousness and circumstances of a violation, VIRLA may take one or more actions including:

Guidance.
Warning.
Training requirement.
Corrective action.
Restriction of bookings.
Restriction of platform features.
Suspension.
Earnings review.
Adjustment of affected amounts where legally permissible.
Termination of the Trainer partnership.
Permanent removal from the platform.
Other contractual or legal remedies available to VIRLA.
The response may depend on the seriousness, frequency, evidence and circumstances of the conduct.

35. IMMEDIATE REMOVAL
VIRLA may take immediate action in serious cases involving:

Sexual misconduct.
Serious harassment.
Physical abuse.
Serious threats.
Fraud.
Identity fraud.
Serious client safety violations.
Deliberate client exploitation.
Unauthorized direct client payments.
Serious platform manipulation.
Serious confidentiality breaches.
Other conduct that presents a significant risk to clients or VIRLA.
36. NO RETALIATION
A Trainer must not retaliate against a client, Trainer, employee or other person for:

Making a genuine complaint.
Reporting a safety concern.
Reporting misconduct.
Cooperating with an investigation.
Exercising a legitimate right.
Retaliation may itself constitute a policy violation.

37. TRAINER RESPONSIBILITY FOR ACCURACY
Trainers must ensure that information submitted to VIRLA is accurate.

This includes:

Identity information.
Qualifications.
Certifications.
Availability.
Attendance.
Session completion.
Payment information.
Contact information.
Other application information.
Material misrepresentation may result in rejection, suspension or termination.

38. CHANGES TO VIRLA POLICIES
VIRLA may update its Partner Terms, Professional Standards and operational policies from time to time.

Updates may be made because of:

Platform improvements.
Safety requirements.
Operational changes.
Business requirements.
Changes in applicable law.
New services.
Changes to industry practices.
Improvements to client protection.
Updated policies may be communicated through the VIRLA application or another official VIRLA communication channel.

39. SEPARATE POLICIES
These Partner Terms & Professional Standards should be read together with other applicable VIRLA policies, including:

VIRLA Earnings & Payout Policy.
VIRLA Trainer Application & Review Policy.
VIRLA Partner Policies / Platform Rules.
VIRLA Privacy Policy.
VIRLA Client Safety Policy.
VIRLA Trainer Equipment & Brand Guidelines.
Any applicable Trainer Agreement.
Other policies communicated by VIRLA.
A Trainer is responsible for complying with all applicable VIRLA policies.

40. NO GUARANTEE OF CONTINUED ACCESS
Approval as a VIRLA Partner Coach does not guarantee continued access to the VIRLA platform.

Continued access depends on:

Compliance with VIRLA policies.
Professional conduct.
Client safety.
Availability and reliability.
Verification requirements.
Platform integrity.
Other applicable eligibility requirements.
41. INDEPENDENT RESPONSIBILITIES
The Trainer is responsible for managing their own professional obligations, qualifications, tax obligations, registrations and other legal responsibilities applicable to their circumstances.

Nothing in these Terms should be interpreted as creating a relationship that is different from the relationship expressly established by the applicable VIRLA agreement and applicable law.

42. APPLICABLE LAW
These Terms are intended to operate subject to applicable laws and regulations of India.

Nothing in these Terms is intended to remove or restrict any right or obligation that cannot legally be excluded or restricted.

43. ACCEPTANCE
By checking the acceptance box, the Trainer confirms:

"I have read and understood the VIRLA Partner Terms & Professional Standards and agree to comply with the applicable professional, safety, client-protection, confidentiality and platform requirements."

44. FINAL ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
The Trainer understands that VIRLA's platform is built around trust, safety, professionalism and reliable service.

The Trainer agrees to:

Put client safety first.
Maintain professional standards.
Follow VIRLA policies.
Protect client information.
Respect client boundaries.
Maintain accurate information.
Attend accepted bookings responsibly.
Use the VIRLA platform honestly.
Avoid unauthorized direct dealings with VIRLA clients.
Cooperate with legitimate VIRLA reviews and investigations.
Failure to comply may result in corrective action, suspension or termination of the VIRLA Partner relationship depending on the circumstances.
