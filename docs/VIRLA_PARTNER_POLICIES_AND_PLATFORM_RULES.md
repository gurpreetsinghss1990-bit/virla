OFFICIAL PARTNER POLICY DOCUMENT
VIRLA — PARTNER POLICIES & PLATFORM RULES
Version: 1.0  |  Applicable To: VIRLA Partner Coaches / Trainers
1. PURPOSE
These VIRLA Partner Policies & Platform Rules establish the operational rules that every VIRLA Partner Coach must follow while using the VIRLA platform and providing services to VIRLA clients.

These rules are designed to ensure:

Reliable sessions.
Safe client experiences.
Professional Trainer behaviour.
Accurate booking and attendance records.
Proper use of VIRLA technology.
Protection of clients and Trainers.
Protection of VIRLA's platform and brand.
Fair and transparent platform operations.
These Policies should be read together with the VIRLA Earnings & Payout Policy and VIRLA Partner Terms & Professional Standards.

2. USING THE VIRLA PLATFORM
Every Trainer must use the VIRLA application and related VIRLA systems honestly and responsibly.

The Trainer must:

Maintain an active and secure account.
Keep their profile information accurate.
Maintain accurate availability.
Accept bookings responsibly.
Keep session information accurate.
Use the VIRLA platform for VIRLA bookings.
Follow all applicable VIRLA instructions.
Keep the application reasonably updated where required.
The Trainer must not attempt to manipulate, interfere with, bypass or misuse any VIRLA platform functionality.

3. TRAINER ACCOUNT
Each VIRLA Trainer account belongs to the verified Trainer to whom it was issued.

A Trainer must not:

Share their account with another person.
Allow another person to conduct sessions under their account.
Allow another person to accept bookings under their identity.
Transfer their account to another person.
Create duplicate accounts without VIRLA authorization.
Use another Trainer's account.
Any unauthorized account sharing or substitution may result in immediate review, suspension or termination.

4. PROFILE INFORMATION
Trainers must keep their VIRLA profile accurate and current.

This includes:

Name.
Photograph.
Mobile number.
Address.
Qualifications.
Specializations.
Certifications.
Availability.
Bank details.
UPI details.
Other required information.
Trainers must immediately update VIRLA where important information changes.

False or misleading information may result in suspension or termination.

5. TRAINER SPECIALIZATIONS
A Trainer may only provide VIRLA workout categories for which they have been approved.

Current VIRLA workout categories may include:

Strength.
Yoga.
Rhythm Dance.
Boxing.
Aerial Yoga.
Other categories officially introduced by VIRLA.
A Trainer must not represent themselves as approved for a category that has not been approved for their account.

Where VIRLA requires additional certification for a workout category, the Trainer must complete that certification before providing the service.

6. TRAINER AVAILABILITY
Trainers are responsible for maintaining accurate availability on the VIRLA platform.

Availability should represent the Trainer's genuine ability to accept and complete sessions.

Trainers should not:

Mark themselves available when they cannot reasonably accept bookings.
Accept overlapping sessions.
Accept bookings they already know they cannot complete.
Deliberately manipulate availability to obtain bookings.
Repeated availability manipulation may affect the Trainer's platform status.

7. ACCEPTING BOOKINGS
Once a Trainer accepts a booking, the Trainer is expected to make reasonable efforts to complete the session.

Before accepting a booking, the Trainer should consider:

Date.
Time.
Client location.
Travel time.
Workout category.
Required equipment.
Personal availability.
Ability to safely conduct the session.
A Trainer should accept only bookings they genuinely expect to fulfil.

8. SESSION TIMING
Trainers should arrive at the client's designated location with sufficient time to begin the session as scheduled.

Trainers are responsible for allowing reasonable travel time between sessions.

Repeated late arrivals may affect:

Client experience.
Trainer performance status.
Booking eligibility.
Incentive eligibility.
Continued access to the platform.
9. TRAINER ARRIVAL AND LOCATION
For location-based sessions, the Trainer must use the VIRLA-provided client location and navigation information.

The Trainer must not intentionally navigate to an unrelated location or deliberately misuse client location information.

Where the Trainer cannot locate the client:

Check the address and map information provided by VIRLA.
Contact the client through the approved VIRLA communication method.
Contact VIRLA support where necessary.
Do not disclose or misuse the client's address.
10. LOCATION AND SESSION VERIFICATION
Where VIRLA uses location verification or session tracking technology, the Trainer must use the system honestly.

The Trainer must not:

Fake their location.
Manipulate GPS information.
Use another person's device to falsely represent their location.
Attempt to bypass location verification.
Manipulate session-start or session-completion information.
Any deliberate attempt to manipulate location or session verification may be treated as a serious platform violation.

11. LIVE LOCATION TRACKING
Where VIRLA's live Trainer-location feature is active for a booking, the Trainer may be required to allow the VIRLA application to use appropriate location information for the relevant session.

Location information is intended to support legitimate VIRLA functions such as:

Trainer arrival.
Client safety.
Session coordination.
Navigation.
Booking verification.
Operational support.
Trainers must not intentionally disable, manipulate or falsify location information for the purpose of bypassing VIRLA's operational systems.

Location permissions and processing will remain subject to VIRLA's applicable privacy requirements and applicable law.

12. CLIENT ADDRESS INFORMATION
Client addresses and location information are confidential.

Trainers must only use client location information for legitimate VIRLA session purposes.

Trainers must not:

Share client addresses.
Photograph client addresses for unrelated purposes.
Publish client locations.
Store client location information unnecessarily.
Use client addresses for unauthorized personal business.
Visit a client's location outside an authorized VIRLA purpose.
13. SESSION START
Trainers must follow VIRLA's designated session-start process.

Where the VIRLA application requires:

Arrival confirmation.
Location confirmation.
Session start confirmation.
Client confirmation.
the Trainer must complete the process accurately.

A Trainer must not falsely start a session before actually commencing the session.

14. SESSION COMPLETION
A Trainer must only mark a session as completed after the session has genuinely been completed according to VIRLA's applicable requirements.

The Trainer must not:

Mark cancelled sessions as completed.
Mark missed sessions as completed.
Mark incomplete sessions as fully completed.
Ask clients to falsely confirm completion.
Manipulate completion records.
15. SESSION QUALITY
Trainers are expected to provide sessions consistent with:

Their approved specialization.
Their VIRLA training.
Their certification.
The client's selected workout.
VIRLA professional standards.
Applicable safety requirements.
Trainers should arrive prepared with the appropriate knowledge and equipment required for the session.

16. CLIENT EXPERIENCE
Trainers should provide a welcoming and professional experience.

Trainers should:

Introduce themselves professionally.
Confirm the client's selected workout where appropriate.
Understand the client's comfort level.
Explain exercises clearly.
Provide appropriate modifications.
Encourage but not pressure the client.
Respect client feedback.
End sessions professionally.
17. CLIENT CONSENT
Client consent must be respected throughout the session.

A Trainer must not pressure a client to:

Perform an exercise they are uncomfortable with.
Receive physical assistance they do not want.
Continue an activity after requesting to stop.
Share personal information unnecessarily.
Participate in photography or video recording without appropriate permission.
18. WORKOUT MODIFICATION
A Trainer may reasonably modify an exercise where necessary for:

Client comfort.
Safety.
Fitness level.
Available equipment.
Space limitations.
Other legitimate session considerations.
However, modifications should remain within the Trainer's approved competence and specialization.

19. MEDICAL AND HEALTH LIMITATIONS
Trainers are not permitted to diagnose medical conditions unless they are separately qualified and legally authorized to do so.

Trainers must not represent themselves as doctors, physiotherapists, medical practitioners or other regulated professionals unless appropriately qualified and authorized.

If a client presents a situation that appears to require medical attention, the Trainer should recommend appropriate professional medical assistance.

20. EMERGENCIES
If a serious emergency occurs during a session, the Trainer should prioritize immediate safety.

Where appropriate:

Stop the activity.
Assist the client within the Trainer's competence.
Contact emergency services where necessary.
Contact the appropriate emergency contact where appropriate.
Notify VIRLA as soon as reasonably possible.
The Trainer must cooperate with any subsequent VIRLA incident review.

21. CLIENT COMMUNICATION
Trainers should use VIRLA-approved communication channels for VIRLA-related communication wherever available.

Communication should remain:

Professional.
Respectful.
Relevant.
Appropriate.
Trainers must not use VIRLA communication systems to harass, pressure, threaten, solicit or exploit clients.

22. PERSONAL CONTACT DETAILS
Trainers should not request or exchange personal contact information with clients for the purpose of bypassing VIRLA.

This includes:

Personal phone numbers.
Personal UPI IDs.
Personal bank details.
Personal social-media accounts.
Private messaging accounts.
Where communication is necessary for an authorized VIRLA session, the Trainer should use VIRLA-approved communication methods.

23. DIRECT PAYMENTS
All VIRLA-related payments must follow VIRLA's approved payment process.

Trainers must not request or accept unauthorized direct payment from VIRLA clients.

This includes:

Cash.
Personal UPI.
Bank transfer.
Personal payment links.
Other private payment methods.
Clients should be directed to the VIRLA platform for VIRLA-related payments.

24. OFF-PLATFORM SESSIONS
Trainers must not arrange unauthorized private sessions with clients obtained through VIRLA.

A Trainer must not:

Ask a VIRLA client to book privately.
Offer a discounted private session to bypass VIRLA.
Move a VIRLA booking outside the platform.
Encourage cancellation of a VIRLA booking for a private session.
Use VIRLA customer information to build an unauthorized private client list.
This rule applies to both paid and unpaid arrangements where the purpose is to bypass VIRLA's platform relationship.

25. CANCELLATIONS BY TRAINERS
Trainers should avoid cancelling accepted bookings except where genuinely necessary.

Where cancellation becomes unavoidable, the Trainer should notify VIRLA as soon as possible through the designated system.

Repeated or avoidable cancellations may result in:

Warning.
Reduced booking access.
Loss of applicable incentives.
Performance review.
Suspension.
Termination.
26. CLIENT CANCELLATIONS
Trainers must follow VIRLA's official cancellation procedure.

Trainers must not independently negotiate cancellation payments or compensation with clients.

Any applicable cancellation charges, credits or refunds should be handled through VIRLA's official system.

27. NO-SHOWS
A Trainer must not falsely report a client as a no-show.

Where a client does not appear:

Follow the applicable VIRLA waiting procedure.
Attempt approved contact where appropriate.
Record the situation accurately.
Follow VIRLA's no-show process.
Trainers must not manipulate no-show records to obtain an unauthorized payment.

28. RATINGS AND REVIEWS
Client ratings and reviews are an important part of the VIRLA platform.

Trainers must not:

Pressure clients to provide positive ratings.
Threaten clients over negative ratings.
Offer benefits in exchange for positive ratings.
Create fake reviews.
Ask another person to submit a false review.
Manipulate the rating system.
Trainers should treat legitimate client feedback professionally.

29. CLIENT COMPLAINTS
If a client raises a complaint, the Trainer should remain professional and cooperate with VIRLA's review.

The Trainer must not:

Threaten the client.
Pressure the client to withdraw the complaint.
Retaliate.
Delete or manipulate relevant evidence.
Misrepresent what occurred.
VIRLA may request information from the Trainer during the review.

30. EVIDENCE AND DOCUMENTATION
Where reasonably required, Trainers should preserve relevant information connected with a complaint, dispute or incident.

This may include:

Booking information.
Session information.
Approved communications.
Relevant timestamps.
Other legitimate evidence.
Trainers must not fabricate, alter or deliberately destroy evidence relating to a VIRLA investigation.

31. TRAINER EQUIPMENT
Trainers must use equipment appropriate for the session and within their competence.

Where VIRLA requires specific equipment or branded items, the Trainer must follow the applicable VIRLA equipment requirements.

Trainers are responsible for maintaining required equipment in safe and usable condition.

32. VIRLA-BRANDED ITEMS
Where VIRLA requires branded Trainer apparel or accessories, Trainers must use them according to VIRLA's applicable Brand Guidelines.

Trainers must not:

Alter VIRLA branding in a misleading way.
Use VIRLA branding for unauthorized commercial activities.
Represent an unauthorized person as a VIRLA Trainer.
33. PERSONAL BELONGINGS
Trainers should take reasonable care of their personal belongings while providing VIRLA services.

Trainers should not ask clients to store valuable Trainer property unless necessary.

34. CLIENT PROPERTY
Trainers must respect client property.

A Trainer must not:

Take client property.
Use client property without permission.
Borrow valuable property without clear permission.
Retain property belonging to a client.
If property is accidentally left behind, the Trainer should follow the appropriate VIRLA procedure.

35. GIFTS AND FAVOURS
Trainers must not pressure clients to provide:

Money.
Gifts.
Personal favours.
Discounts.
Services.
Other benefits.
Any significant or unusual gift or benefit should be disclosed to VIRLA where required by applicable policy.

36. COMPETING SERVICES
Trainers must not use VIRLA's client information, booking information or platform access to promote unauthorized competing services directly to VIRLA clients.

Trainers may conduct other legitimate professional activities outside VIRLA, provided those activities do not breach their VIRLA agreements, confidentiality obligations or client-circumvention requirements.

37. SOCIAL MEDIA AND CONTENT
Trainers must obtain appropriate permission before publishing client photographs, videos, testimonials or other identifiable client content.

Trainers must not publish:

Client addresses.
Private client information.
Confidential VIRLA information.
Internal VIRLA systems.
Private booking information.
Screenshots containing confidential data.
38. RECORDING SESSIONS
A Trainer must not secretly record VIRLA sessions.

Any recording of a client must comply with applicable consent, privacy and VIRLA requirements.

Where VIRLA creates official training or marketing content, Trainers must follow the applicable VIRLA media process.

39. PLATFORM SECURITY
Trainers must not attempt to bypass VIRLA's technical or security controls.

Prohibited activities include:

Hacking.
Unauthorized database access.
Credential theft.
Security bypass.
Exploiting application vulnerabilities.
Manipulating API requests for unauthorized purposes.
Accessing another user's information.
Reverse-engineering systems for malicious purposes.
Any suspected security vulnerability should be reported to VIRLA rather than exploited.

40. FRAUD AND MANIPULATION
Trainers must not manipulate VIRLA's systems for financial or operational benefit.

This includes:

Fake bookings.
Fake sessions.
Fake attendance.
Duplicate accounts.
False location information.
False client confirmations.
False cancellation claims.
Payment manipulation.
Incentive manipulation.
Referral manipulation.
Any other fraudulent activity.
Fraud may result in immediate suspension or termination.

41. REFERRALS AND PROMOTIONS
Where VIRLA offers referral programs, promotional campaigns or Trainer incentives, Trainers must follow the specific rules applicable to each program.

Trainers must not:

Create fake referrals.
Refer themselves through another account.
Create duplicate accounts.
Manipulate promotional codes.
Misrepresent promotional eligibility.
42. CONFIDENTIAL VIRLA INFORMATION
Trainers may receive information about VIRLA that is not publicly available.

This may include:

Internal procedures.
Business information.
Pricing structures.
Internal Trainer information.
Client information.
Technology information.
Operational information.
Unreleased products or features.
Trainers must not disclose confidential VIRLA information without authorization.

43. DATA PROTECTION
Trainers must handle personal information responsibly.

Personal information obtained through VIRLA must only be used for legitimate VIRLA-related purposes.

Trainers should not store personal client information longer than reasonably necessary for the authorized purpose.

44. ACCOUNT SECURITY
Trainers must keep their VIRLA login information secure.

If a Trainer believes their account has been compromised, they should notify VIRLA promptly.

VIRLA may temporarily restrict access while investigating a security concern.

45. APP AND DEVICE REQUIREMENTS
Trainers must maintain a device capable of reasonably supporting the VIRLA application and required functionality.

Trainers should:

Maintain sufficient battery during sessions.
Maintain a reasonable internet connection where required.
Keep the VIRLA application updated where required.
Allow necessary device permissions.
Ensure location services are available where required for location-based sessions.
Trainers should plan accordingly before accepting sessions.

46. TECHNICAL PROBLEMS
If the VIRLA application experiences a technical problem during a booking, the Trainer should:

Avoid manipulating the application to bypass the problem.
Record relevant details.
Contact VIRLA support where appropriate.
Follow VIRLA's instructions.
Provide accurate information about what occurred.
Technical problems must not be deliberately used to obtain unauthorized earnings.

47. SERVICE AREA
Trainers may only accept sessions within the areas and locations for which they are eligible or authorized under VIRLA's current operating model.

VIRLA may change service areas based on:

Client demand.
Trainer availability.
Operational requirements.
Safety.
Business expansion.
Other legitimate considerations.
48. CLIENT LOCATION SAFETY
Where a Trainer is required to travel to a client's location, the Trainer should exercise reasonable personal safety precautions.

If the Trainer reasonably believes a location or situation is unsafe, they should:

Avoid entering an unsafe environment.
Move to a safe location where possible.
Contact VIRLA.
Follow the applicable safety procedure.
The Trainer should not knowingly expose themselves or the client to unnecessary danger.

49. PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES
A Trainer must not conduct the following activities through VIRLA unless specifically authorized and legally permitted:

Illegal activities.
Unauthorized medical treatment.
Sexual services.
Harassment.
Violence.
Threats.
Fraud.
Unauthorized financial transactions.
Sale of prohibited substances.
Any activity that violates applicable law.
Any activity that materially compromises client or platform safety.
50. PROFESSIONAL APPEARANCE
Trainers are expected to maintain an appropriate professional appearance during VIRLA sessions.

This includes:

Appropriate clothing.
Appropriate hygiene.
Clean and professional appearance.
Required VIRLA-branded apparel where applicable.
Appropriate footwear and equipment.
51. TRAINER SUBSTITUTION
A Trainer may not send another person to conduct a VIRLA session in their place unless VIRLA has expressly authorized the substitution.

An unauthorized substitute session may be treated as a serious platform violation.

52. PLATFORM COMMUNICATIONS
Trainers are expected to read important VIRLA communications relating to:

Bookings.
Policy changes.
Safety.
Training.
Payments.
Platform updates.
Operational requirements.
Failure to review important communications does not automatically excuse a violation of applicable requirements.

53. POLICY VIOLATIONS
If VIRLA identifies a potential violation, it may review the relevant circumstances.

Depending on the seriousness of the issue, VIRLA may take actions including:

Guidance.
Warning.
Training.
Corrective action.
Restriction of bookings.
Feature restrictions.
Temporary suspension.
Earnings review where applicable.
Termination.
Permanent removal.
54. SERIOUS VIOLATIONS
Serious violations may result in immediate suspension while VIRLA conducts an appropriate review.

Examples include:

Serious client safety violations.
Sexual misconduct.
Physical abuse.
Threats.
Fraud.
Identity fraud.
Unauthorized direct payments.
Deliberate client circumvention.
Serious confidentiality breaches.
Account sharing.
Deliberate location manipulation.
Deliberate session manipulation.
Serious platform security violations.
55. COOPERATION WITH VIRLA
Trainers must cooperate reasonably with legitimate VIRLA investigations, audits and operational reviews.

Trainers must provide truthful information and must not deliberately obstruct an investigation.

56. NO RETALIATION
A Trainer must not retaliate against a client, Trainer, VIRLA employee or other person for making a genuine complaint, reporting a safety concern or cooperating with an investigation.

Retaliation may result in disciplinary action.

57. POLICY CHANGES
VIRLA may update these Partner Policies & Platform Rules from time to time.

Updates may be introduced due to:

Platform changes.
New services.
Safety improvements.
Operational requirements.
Changes in applicable law.
Business requirements.
Technology changes.
Updated policies may be communicated through the VIRLA application or another official VIRLA communication channel.

58. SEPARATE VIRLA POLICIES
These Platform Rules should be read together with:

VIRLA Earnings & Payout Policy.
VIRLA Partner Terms & Professional Standards.
VIRLA Trainer Application & Review Policy.
VIRLA Privacy Policy.
VIRLA Client Safety Policy.
VIRLA Trainer Equipment & Brand Guidelines.
Any applicable Trainer Agreement.
Other applicable VIRLA policies.
59. NO GUARANTEE OF CONTINUED ACCESS
Compliance with these rules is a condition of continued participation as a VIRLA Partner Coach.

VIRLA may restrict, suspend or terminate access where permitted under the applicable Trainer agreement, policies and law.

60. ACCEPTANCE
By checking the acceptance box, the Trainer confirms:

"I have read, understood, and agree to comply with the VIRLA Partner Policies & Platform Rules."

61. FINAL ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
The Trainer understands that VIRLA's platform depends on:

Client safety.
Professional conduct.
Reliable sessions.
Accurate information.
Honest platform usage.
Respect for client privacy.
Respect for Trainer responsibilities.
Protection of the VIRLA brand.
Compliance with VIRLA policies.
The Trainer agrees to follow these requirements while participating as a VIRLA Partner Coach.
