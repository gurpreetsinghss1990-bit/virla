Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Virla, operated by Namratha Jauni, may collect, use and protect information provided by customers and trainers while using Virla services.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Virla, information may include your name, mobile number, email address, location, fitness preferences, booking information and payment or transaction details.

How We Use Information

We may use information to create and manage accounts, facilitate trainer discovery and bookings, process payments, provide customer support, communicate service updates, improve our services and meet applicable legal obligations.

Payments

Online payments may be processed by authorised third-party payment service providers. Virla does not intend to store complete payment-card credentials on its own systems.

Sharing

Information may be shared with trainers, service providers, payment partners or other parties where reasonably necessary to provide Virla services, prevent fraud, provide support or comply with law.

Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may contact Virla to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Virla customer support using the contact details published on our Contact page.
