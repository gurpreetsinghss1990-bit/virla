## Brand & Style

This design system blends high-performance athletic vigor with private concierge wellness. Designed for discerning mobile users booking vetted personal fitness coaches and wellness specialists to their personal residences, the visual language balances urgent athletic motivation with immaculate hospitality trust.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with **Tonal Glassmorphism**:
- Crisp, warm rose-tinted neutral canvases (`#FCF5F5` / `#fff8f8`) paired with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) elevated cards to evoke cleanliness and hospitality.
- Deep obsidian and onyx surfaces (`#09090B` / `#101828`) to deliver an athletic, premium, high-contrast punch across navigation headers and authentication cards.
- Bold athletic crimson-red (`#E11D48` / `#b80035`) deployed deliberately as the primary kinetic trigger for actions, active tab accents, and status focal points.
- Warm amber/gold highlights (`#F59E0B` / `#FEF3C7`) drawn from the brand mark to celebrate trainer ratings, verified tiers, and user progress achievements.

## Colors

The palette pairs high-energy chromatic punches with deeply grounded neutral foundations:

- **Primary (`#E11D48` / `#b80035`)**: High-saturation athletic crimson. Reserved strictly for primary action buttons, FAB interactive states, active tab highlights, and urgent status indicators. Never dilute its presence by using it on massive static background blocks.
- **Secondary (`#101828` / `#565e71`)**: Deep obsidian-slate. Serves as the principal typography color, primary header anchor, and structural contrast boundary.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` / `#815100`)**: Radiant amber-gold inspired by the brand mark. Used for trainer reviews, verified gold tier badges, streak milestones, and star ratings.
- **Neutral (`#FCF5F5` / `#fff8f8`)**: Warm rose-tinted off-white foundation canvas. Softens mobile glare while maintaining clean separation from pure white surface cards.

### Functional Roles
- **Verified Emerald (`#059669`)** & **Mint Surface (`#ECFDF5`)**: Used for background-checked coach verification pills, completed milestones, and upcoming session badges.
- **Obsidian Auth Surface (`#0F0F12`)**: Dark glassmorphic layer used during onboarding, splash sequences, and authentication overlays.

## Typography

The typography system pairs **Outfit** for structural headings and athletic impact with **Inter** for dense transactional UI legibility across iOS and Android screens:

- **Display & Headlines (Outfit)**: Modern, clean geometric sans-serif with strong geometric curves. Delivers bold sports motivation and concierge polish across hero banners, trainer nameplates, and screen titles.
- **Body & Captions (Inter)**: High x-height and neutral grotesque clarity engineered specifically for small screen readability during rapid scanning of booking times, addresses, and exercise programs.
- **Eyebrow & Badges (`label-sm`)**: Formatted in all-caps with `0.08em` letter tracking to indicate categories (e.g., `CONCIERGE ON-DEMAND`, `STRENGTH & MOBILITY`, `CONFIRMED`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a **mobile-first fluid column system** strictly governed by a 4px base increment:

- **Mobile Viewports (<600px)**: Single column with a fixed `margin` of `1rem` (16px) left and right. Main content cards span edge-to-edge minus the canvas margins. Internal card paddings default to `1rem` to `1.5rem`.
- **Tablet / Large Handhelds (>=600px)**: 8 to 12-column layout with `margin-tablet` of `1.5rem` (24px) and max content width constrained to `800px` centered to preserve editorial focus.
- **Vertical Navigation Clearance**: Scroll containers must provide bottom insets accounting for system gesture areas plus the floating glass navigation bar (minimum 80px on Android, 96px on iOS).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined combination of **tonal layering**, **hairline borders**, and **ambient tinted shadows**:

- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Warm rose-tinted neutral (`#FCF5F5` / `#fff8f8`) flat background.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) cards resting atop Level 0. Outlined with a soft 1px border (`#E5E7EB` at 60% opacity) and an ambient obsidian drop shadow (`rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.04)` blur 16px, offset y: 6px).
- **Level 2 (Floating Controls & Bottom Bar)**: Translucent glassmorphic pills (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with 20px blur) framed by a crisp 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`), casting a deeper grounding shadow (`rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.08)` blur 20px, offset y: 8px).
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Overlays)**: Crisp white sheets with 28px top radii over a dark semi-transparent obsidian scrim (`rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.60)`).
- **Athletic FAB Glow**: The central quick-book floating action button carries an energetic red ambient aura (`rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.35)` blur 12px, offset y: 4px).

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape vocabulary with athletic pill highlights:

- **Standard Containers & Cards**: Defined at `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) to `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for cards, dialogs, and trainer profiles.
- **Interactive Controls & Inputs**: Form fields and primary action buttons utilize `1rem` (16px) corner rounding to ensure a modern, ergonomic thumb-press zone.
- **Chips, Badges & Action Pills**: Full pill border radii (`9999px`) are reserved for workout tags, status pills, category pills, and floating navigation bars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Athletic Button**: Solid Crimson-Red (`#E11D48`) fill with crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`, `label-lg`). Minimum touch height of 56px, horizontal padding `1.5rem`, rounded `1rem`. Active state scales down to `0.98` with darkened crimson feedback (`#C0003C`).
- **Secondary Obsidian Button**: Deep Obsidian (`#101828`) background with white text, ideal for secondary booking options or view-details prompts.
- **Concierge Ghost Button**: Transparent fill, 1px hairline border (`#E5E7EB`), text in `#101828`. Hover and press states tint with `#FFF1F2`.

### Cards & Modules
- **Trainer Profile Card**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surface with 24px rounded corners and 1px hairline border. Contains a circular trainer avatar (56px) with an emerald verification badge, full name (`headline-sm`), warm amber star rating (`#F59E0B`), distance metric, and pill tags for specialties.
- **Upcoming Session Card**: White card highlighted by a 4px left-border accent in Crimson-Red (`#E11D48`) or Emerald (`#059669`), displaying session start countdown, trainer detail snippet, and an inline one-tap reschedule or direct contact trigger.

### Input Fields
- **Mobile Number & Verification Inputs**: High-contrast, dark-tinted or crisp white containers with 16px corner radii. Focus states illuminate with a 1.5px Crimson-Red (`#E11D48`) outline and subtle glow. Prefix country-code pills (`#101828` or `#F4F4F5`) sit flush or nested within the bounding container with bold white or obsidian text.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Specialty & Filter Chips**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height 36px, horizontal padding `1rem`. Unselected chips use white fill with `#E5E7EB` borders and `#475569` text. Selected chips adopt an emerald tint (`#ECFDF5`) with `#059669` border and text, or soft crimson (`#FFF1F2`) with `#E11D48` text depending on context.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded squares (8px) for checkboxes and full circles for radio selectors. Unchecked state uses a 1.5px `#E5E7EB` border. Selected state fills with Athletic Crimson-Red (`#E11D48`) holding a crisp white checkmark or center dot.

### Floating Bottom Navigation Bar
- Frosted translucent pill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with backdrop filter blur 24px) floating 16px above the home indicator. Features active tab icons in Crimson-Red (`#E11D48`) and an elevated circular FAB at the center for instant trainer booking.
