Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective date: 24 August 2026

Virla, operated by Namratha Jauni, provides fitness training services through scheduled bookings. This policy explains the general approach to cancellations, rescheduling and refunds.

Customer Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation or rescheduling through the available Virla support or booking channels. Eligibility for a refund may depend on how far in advance the request is made and the applicable booking terms.

Trainer Cancellation

If a trainer cancels or Virla is unable to provide a confirmed session, Virla may offer rescheduling or an eligible refund, depending on the circumstances.

Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, it will normally be initiated through the original payment method or payment channel, subject to the processing timelines of the relevant payment provider or financial institution.

Non-Refundable Circumstances

Refunds may not be available for sessions missed by a customer without timely notice or where the booking terms specifically state otherwise.

Support

For a cancellation, refund or payment query, contact Virla customer support with your booking and transaction details.
